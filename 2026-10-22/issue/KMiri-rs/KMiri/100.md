## Body

When main thread switches to a task, `kernel_task_entry` enters, making the call stack a fresh new one, the debugger still runs.

But when  `kernel_task_entry` returns, the debugger successfully exits, while it should jump back to the main thread (or jump to another task, though multiple users tasks haven't been tested yet).
