Rust libc fork of BlueOS is old to gate two c_void types:
https://github.com/KMiri-rs/vivoblueos-libc/blob/f83317852f1825bd6eb2a47f12b790129c5ab7a5/src/unix/mod.rs#L1686-L1704
But the latest Rust libc use the libcore's c_void type:
https://github.com/rust-lang/libc/blob/b739c733e06a5f184bae7e296919c15d6ccd8ec8/src/macros.rs#L154
