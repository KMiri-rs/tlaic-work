## Body

The nixfmt check is a part of `make check`:
https://github.com/asterinas/asterinas/blob/4d395b88506ebe643fcfd51ced81bda48ee2effd/Makefile#L489

But it warns:

```bash
$ nixfmt --check ./distro
  Passing directories or non-Nix files (such as "./distro") is deprecated and will be unsupported soon. Please use the `pkgs.nixfmt-tree` wrapper instead, or https://github.com/numtide/treefmt-nix for more flexibility
```
