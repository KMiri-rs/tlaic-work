## Body

> In fact, I think we discussed how to (automatically?) manage the version of an in-tree crate if we want to publish the crate but not keep its version in sync with Asterinas's version (e.g., 0.18.0) in a community meeting several months ago. But we don't reach a very good solution at the time as well. I am not sure if we should consider this.
>
> Commented in https://github.com/asterinas/asterinas/issues/3724#issuecomment-5762985777

Cargo book suggests several tools for auto crate publishing.

https://doc.rust-lang.org/cargo/reference/publishing.html#publishing-a-new-version-of-an-existing-crate

But I have zero experience with them.
