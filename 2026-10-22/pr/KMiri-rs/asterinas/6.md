## Body

Fix https://github.com/KMiri-rs/KMiri/issues/57 by two kmiri shims
* `kern_miri_zero` initializes the page with zeros, which is necessary for the initial `PteScalar::Absent` state in the following call of map_base_page
* `kern_miri_retype_pages` retypes a PageTable with PTE size (exactly 8 bytes in Asterinas)

Also fix the `ptr.add` UB with integer arithmetic.
