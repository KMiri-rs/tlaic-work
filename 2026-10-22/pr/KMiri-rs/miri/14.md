Fix https://github.com/KMiri-rs/KMiri/issues/51

`Cell::default` and `Cell::new("")` now differs since https://github.com/ratatui/ratatui/pull/2150:
* `default` has zero column_span, which doesn't render the cell at all
* `new` has one column_span, which renders the cell as a column like before
