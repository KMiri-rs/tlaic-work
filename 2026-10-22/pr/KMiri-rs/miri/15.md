## Body

As a workaround to fix https://github.com/KMiri-rs/KMiri/issues/49

The kernel stack size was increased to 50 MB; its placement was also shifted to follow the free pages, whereas it previously preceded them.

The stack usage comes from 0xffffffff89f63a67 to 0xffffffff8b200000 (vaddr), consuming 18.61 MB.
