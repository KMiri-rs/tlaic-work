## Body

close https://github.com/KMiri-rs/KMiri/issues/83

Stack range of a new task is `0xffffdffffff7e000..0xffffdfffffffe000`, while the vaddr is linear computed via paddr, causing the out-of-bound UB. 

So a hot fix is saving the non-linear mapping from paddr to vaddr, and the correct vaddr is used in page_walk.
